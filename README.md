Sum up 21 November's world news in one image: a cream broadsheet front page for Saturday 21 November 2026 with 46 numbered stories from every region — COP31 in overtime in Antalya, Iran's attack on US bases in Bahrain, the capture of cartel kingpin Chapo Isidro in Sinaloa — 15 of them illustrated with drawn scenes, every illustrated card carrying a trust chip and a named source, plus a UTC timeline, the day's six big numbers, a region strip with counts, a dotted world map with numbered pins, and a red DATE CHECK box listing the big stories deliberately excluded because they were first reported on 19-20 November or on 22 November.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes entry.png (1024x1024, deterministic: two runs are byte-identical). The poster is drawn by draw.py, kept in this repo as the build source.
